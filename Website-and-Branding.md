# L’Ère du Verseau — Website & Branding

A Framer website for an art and writing collective.

[View the live website](https://reliable-designers-916768.framer.app/)

## My role

I created and manage the website, including its design, navigation, branding, maintenance, and ongoing refinements. Artwork creation and artwork uploading are handled separately.

## Design and build process

I worked with ChatGPT to develop the concept, clarify design choices, and prepare prompts for Framer. I applied changes in Framer, reviewed the result, and refined the next prompt to improve the site.

The website brings together visual art, poetry, literary articles, places we knew, and diary entries. Its pale aqua header, cream background, brown serif typography, and pouring-vessel logo give those sections a consistent visual identity.

## CRAFT prompt engineering

I used **CRAFT — Context, Role, Audience, Format, and Tone** — to make my requests specific and repeatable.

| Element | Application |
| --- | --- |
| Context | Explain the collective, current design, and intended change. |
| Role | Give ChatGPT a design or branding role. |
| Audience | Consider readers exploring the collective’s art and writing. |
| Format | Request a design direction, logo variation, or Framer instructions. |
| Tone | Maintain the project’s reflective, literary character. |

Reviewing each result helped me identify what to clarify in the next prompt.

## Logo and visual identity

I developed the Aquarius-inspired pouring vessel and wordmark with ChatGPT. The logo work explored primary and stacked layouts, a standalone wordmark and pitcher, a monogram, a circular seal, sepia and reversed versions, a favicon, and an Aquarius accent.

I also worked on transparent-background versions for Framer so the designs could sit against different page colors while preserving their proportions.

**Tools:** Framer, ChatGPT, CRAFT prompt engineering.
