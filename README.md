Portfolio case study: building L’Ère du Verseau with Framer and ChatGPT, CRAFT prompt engineering, logo development, and website management.

## L’Ère du Verseau

**Website creation, prompt engineering, brand identity, and ongoing site management**  
**By Charles Mogavero**

[Visit the live website](https://reliable-designers-916768.framer.app/)

### Project overview

I created the L’Ère du Verseau website in Framer, working with ChatGPT throughout the planning, design, and refinement process. The site presents a creative collective through visual art, poetry, literary articles, places we knew, and diary entries, with a homepage featuring a monthly exhibit, recent entries, and a manifesto.

My role covers the website’s design, structure, branding, maintenance, and day-to-day operation. Artwork creation and artwork uploading are handled separately and are outside my role.

### Building and refining the website with ChatGPT and Framer

I used ChatGPT as a collaborator to develop ideas, clarify the project’s direction, and turn my requirements into prompts I could use while building in Framer. I made the design decisions and used Framer to bring the website together.

The process was iterative: describe the intended result, develop and refine the prompt with ChatGPT, apply changes in Framer, review the result, and adjust the next prompt. This helped me move from an initial concept toward a consistent website and visual identity.

The site’s presentation brings together a pale aqua header, a cream background, brown serif typography, and a water-pouring vessel logo. These choices support its reflective, literary character across the different content sections.

### CRAFT prompt engineering

I used the **CRAFT** technique—**Context, Role, Audience, Format, and Tone**—to organize my prompts and communicate the result I wanted more clearly.

| Element | How it guided my prompts |
| --- | --- |
| **Context** | Describe L’Ère du Verseau, the website’s purpose, the current design, and the change I wanted to make. |
| **Role** | Give ChatGPT a clear design or branding role for the task. |
| **Audience** | Keep readers and visitors exploring the collective’s art and writing in mind. |
| **Format** | Specify the kind of output I needed, such as a design direction, a logo variation, or instructions to use in Framer. |
| **Tone** | Keep the language and visual direction aligned with the project’s reflective, literary identity. |

CRAFT gave my prompts a repeatable structure. As I reviewed each result, I could clarify a particular requirement and refine the prompt for the next iteration.

### Logo creation and variations

I developed the logo and its variations with ChatGPT, using an Aquarius-inspired water-pouring vessel and the L’Ère du Verseau wordmark as the central identity.

The logo work explored primary and stacked arrangements, a wordmark, a standalone pitcher, a monogram, a circular seal, sepia and reversed versions, a favicon, and an Aquarius accent. These variations gave me options for different placements and backgrounds.

The preparation process also included transparent-background versions for use in Framer, with the aim of letting the page background show through while preserving the original designs and proportions. This repository documents that work; the logo asset files are not included here.

### Operating and managing the site

I run and manage the website, including its design direction, page structure, branding, maintenance, and ongoing refinements. I continue to use ChatGPT to help express ideas and prepare prompts, and Framer to implement website changes.

**The boundary of my role is artwork creation and artwork uploading: those tasks are handled separately.**
